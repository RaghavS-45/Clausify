DOCUMENT TYPE: Knowledge Base Reference
TOPIC: Questions to Ask Before Signing an Employment Contract — India
APPLICABLE TO: offer_letter, employment_contract, internship_agreement, nda

---

OVERVIEW

Most people sign employment contracts within 24–48 hours of receiving them, often without asking critical questions. This document provides a comprehensive, categorized list of questions you should ask an employer before signing — organized by topic.

Asking these questions is not rude. A good employer will welcome a diligent candidate. If asking clarifying questions about your employment terms is frowned upon, that itself is a red flag about the culture.

---

SECTION 1: COMPENSATION AND CTC

1. Can you provide a full CTC breakup in a separate sheet? (Basic, HRA, Special Allowance, Variable Pay, PF, Gratuity, Insurance breakdown)

2. What are the targets for the variable/performance bonus? Is this measured at individual, team, or company level? What percentage of eligible employees received 100% of their variable pay in the last 2 years?

3. Is the PF contribution calculated on full Basic salary, or is it capped at ₹15,000/month (₹1,800/month employer PF)?

4. When is variable pay disbursed — quarterly, semi-annually, or annually? Is there an "employment on date of payout" condition?

5. Is there a joining bonus? What is the clawback period and rate? Is clawback pro-rated?

6. How often are salary revisions conducted? Is there a documented appraisal process?

7. Is the CTC inclusive of statutory benefits (PF, gratuity), or are those on top of the stated figure?

---

SECTION 2: NOTICE PERIOD

8. What is the notice period on my side? What is the notice period on the company's side? Are they symmetric?

9. Is notice period buyout allowed? Who can initiate it — me, the company, or either party? How is the buyout amount calculated?

10. What is the notice period during probation? Is it specifically stated separately from the post-confirmation notice?

11. Can the company place me on garden leave? Do I continue to receive full salary during garden leave?

12. Can the company waive my notice period and provide early release?

---

SECTION 3: PROBATION AND CONFIRMATION

13. What is the probation period? Is confirmation automatic at the end, or does it require a separate action/letter?

14. Can the probation period be extended? How many times and by how much?

15. What are the grounds for non-confirmation during probation?

16. Does any performance review happen during probation? Is there a structured evaluation?

---

SECTION 4: EMPLOYMENT BOND

17. Is there an employment bond? What is the duration and amount?

18. What specific training does the bond amount represent? Can you share the training cost breakdown?

19. Does the bond amount reduce pro-rata over the bond period? If I serve 12 months of a 24-month bond, is my liability reduced by 50%?

20. Does the bond apply if I am terminated without cause? (It should not.)

---

SECTION 5: NON-COMPETE AND IP

21. Does the contract have a post-employment non-compete clause? What is the duration, geographic scope, and industry scope?

22. Does the IP assignment clause cover work done entirely on personal time with personal equipment, unrelated to the company's business?

23. Can I carve out my existing personal projects and prior inventions before signing? Will you acknowledge this list in writing?

24. Can I contribute to open-source projects during my employment? Is prior approval required?

25. Does the confidentiality clause restrict me from discussing my salary with colleagues?

---

SECTION 6: EQUITY / ESOP

26. How many options or RSUs are being granted? What is the current FMV per share?

27. What is the exercise price (strike price)?

28. What is the vesting schedule — cliff period, monthly or quarterly vesting thereafter?

29. What is the exercise window after I leave the company? (30 days is a red flag; 90 days is standard; best-in-class is 5+ years)

30. Is this company DPIIT-recognized? Does the 5-year ESOP tax deferral under the Finance Act 2020 apply?

31. What is the total fully-diluted share count? What percentage of the company does my grant represent?

32. Is there accelerated vesting in the event of an acquisition or change of control?

33. Can I sell my shares on secondary markets? Is there a right of first refusal for the company?

34. Can I see the full ESOP Policy document?

---

SECTION 7: WORK LOCATION AND WFH

35. What is the official work location? Is it stated in the contract?

36. What is the WFH / remote work policy? Is it contractual or discretionary ("subject to change at company discretion")?

37. Does the relocation clause require my consent, or can the company unilaterally relocate me?

38. If I am relocated, what relocation support does the company provide (allowance, accommodation, notice period before relocation)?

---

SECTION 8: LEAVE AND BENEFITS

39. What is the total leave entitlement? How many days each of earned leave, casual leave, and sick leave?

40. Can unused earned leave be carried over to the next year? What is the maximum carryover?

41. Is leave encashable on exit? How is the encashment amount calculated?

42. What is the health insurance coverage? Sum insured, does it cover family members, and are parents covered (even at additional premium)?

43. What are the maternity and paternity leave policies? Are they compliant with the Maternity Benefit Act (26 weeks for first two children)?

44. Is there a learning and development budget? How much per year, and what is the process for claiming it?

45. What is the group life insurance cover (if any)?

---

SECTION 9: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

46. In which city's courts does the contract place jurisdiction? Is it the city where I will work?

47. Is there an arbitration clause? Who appoints the arbitrator — mutually or by the company alone?

48. Does the company have an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) under the POSH Act?

49. What is the internal grievance resolution process for workplace disputes?

---

SECTION 10: PROCESS AND PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

50. Can I see the full appointment letter / employment agreement BEFORE joining? (Not just the offer letter.)

51. Is the appointment letter standard, or does it contain terms not in the offer letter?

52. What is the background verification process? What happens if a minor discrepancy is found?

53. Are there any other conditions attached to this offer beyond what is stated in the offer letter?

54. What is the onboarding process? Where should I report on Day 1, and what do I need to bring?

55. Is the role included in the ESIC scheme? (Relevant if monthly salary < ₹21,000 — ESIC provides health and disability coverage)

---

HOW TO ASK THESE QUESTIONS

1. Ask most questions via email — creates a written record of the answers.
2. Send a consolidated email rather than many separate ones — shows diligence, not indecision.
3. Frame questions as seeking clarity, not as confrontation: "I want to make sure I understand the compensation structure fully before I sign" — not "Why is your notice period so long?"
4. Give a realistic timeline: "I'd appreciate a response within 3 business days" — this is reasonable.
5. If you are using a recruiter or placement agency, route the technical questions through them with a request that the answers be confirmed in writing.
